The shape of the course

      
41 lessons · 9 modules ·
        573 animated steps · ~13h with practice

    

    

    
One column per module, as tall as the animated steps inside it and banded once
      per lesson — so you can see where the course gets heavy before you start. Click a column
      to jump to that module.

  


  

    
How to actually use this

    
Watch each lesson once at normal speed to see its shape. Then step through it manually a second
    time and try to predict what happens before pressing the button. Predicting is what turns
    watching into knowing. Use the scrubber to jump back to any moment.
