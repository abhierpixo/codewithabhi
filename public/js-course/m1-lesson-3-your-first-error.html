Your first error, and how to read it

We are going to break things on purpose. Red text is not a punishment and it does not mean you're bad at this — it's the computer telling you, quite politely, exactly where it got stuck. By the end of this lesson you'll be able to read any JavaScript error in about ten seconds.

The habit to build

        

          
1Read the typeThe first word tells you what family of problem this is.

          
2Read the messageIt is a real sentence in English, and it is usually literally true.

          
3Go to the lineThen look at that line and the one just above it.

        

        
Do these three things before anything else. Most beginners scroll past the error and start guessing — which is the slowest possible way to fix it.
